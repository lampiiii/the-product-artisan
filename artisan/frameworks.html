The Daily Decision Loop

Four questions, every decision

Before I commit to a piece of work, it runs through this loop — the same four checks from the playbook's Action Framework.

1

Locate the Work

Strategic Apex or Task Execution? If it's execution, can it be delegated or automated?

2

Deepen the Moat

Am I exercising judgment, domain depth, or governance — the skills AI can't replace?

3

Prove the Value

Can this tie to a measurable customer or business outcome — not just output shipped?

4

Zoom to System

Am I thinking across the whole system, or stuck inside one feature?
