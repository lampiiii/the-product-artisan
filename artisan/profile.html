About

Who I am

Senior Product Manager with 5+ years in B2B enterprise software. At a global enterprise SaaS company, I lead all AI features — including AI agents that work on their own — for the Customer Success product. I built a system of 19 AI agents that helped win 10+ competitive deals against Gainsight, while the product's yearly contract revenue grew from $18M to $110M. I also grew our premium subscription tier from 0 to 120 paying accounts by shipping AI features good enough that customers wanted to pay more for them. My focus is building AI tools that give enterprises real, measurable results — without adding more manual work for their teams.

Beyond the product itself, I built an AI-powered operating system for how my own team works. It's a set of 30+ automated helpers, 70+ ready-made routines, and 40+ shortcuts, built on the AI coding tool Claude Code, that handles writing product specs, summarizing research, tracking plans, drafting stakeholder updates, and checking project health — automatically, instead of by hand.

Before product management, I built data pipelines at a Y Combinator-backed startup and started my career in analytics and business operations. I work best where AI, data, and enterprise software meet. I hold an MBA from IIM Udaipur and a B.Tech from IET Lucknow.
