About
Who I am

Senior Product Manager with 5+ years in B2B enterprise software. At a global enterprise SaaS company, I lead all AI features — including AI agents that work on their own — for the Customer Success product.

I built a system of 19 AI agents that helped win 10+ competitive deals against rival vendors, while the product's yearly contract revenue grew from $18M to $110M. I also grew our premium subscription tier from 0 to 120 paying accounts by shipping AI features good enough that customers wanted to pay more for them.

Before product management, I built data pipelines at a Y Combinator-backed startup and started my career in analytics and business operations. I work best where AI, data, and enterprise software meet. I hold an MBA from IIM and a B.Tech from IET Lucknow.
