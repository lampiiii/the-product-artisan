What's actually on my shelf

Not a "best PM books" listicle — this is what I've bought and what I actually follow, grouped by what it's taught me: how to decide under uncertainty, how to read data, how to run a customer success motion, and how to keep showing up.

The Bookshelf

Decisions & data

The mechanics of making a call under uncertainty, and using data to back it instead of a hunch.

Decision-Making

Thinking in Bets

Annie Duke

Treats every decision as a bet made under uncertainty — separating the quality of a choice from whether it happened to work out.

Decision-Making

Behind Every Good Decision

Piyanka Jain & Puneet Sharma

A plain-English framework for turning business questions into data questions a team can actually answer.

Decision-Making

Moneyball

Michael Lewis

Beating conventional wisdom by looking at the numbers everyone else was ignoring.

Data & Analytics

Storytelling with Data

Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic

How to turn a chart into a decision instead of just a picture.

Data & Analytics

HBR Guide to Data Analytics Basics for Managers

Harvard Business Review

A manager's primer for reading and using data without needing to become a data scientist.

Data & Analytics

Data Smart

John Foreman

Data science taught hands-on through spreadsheets, not abstract theory.

The operating playbook for running a customer success function day to day.

Customer Success

Customer Success

A practical guide to building a career and a function in customer success.
